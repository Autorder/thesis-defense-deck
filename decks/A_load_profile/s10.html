An idea for later: a daily HVAC cap

What bothered me

The fixed split packs Haifa’s cooling into a few hot days — up to 578 kWh of HVAC in one day. No home can do that.

The idea

Cap HVAC per day at what the home’s air-conditioners can deliver; move the excess to the base load. The annual budget stays the same.

Scaled to the home

≈1.4 average days of the home: 30 / 45 / 75 kWh at 8k / 12k / 20k.

A first look only: hot cities barely change, Haifa’s peak drops from 112 to ~22 kWh/h. A full answer needs the whole run matrix again.
