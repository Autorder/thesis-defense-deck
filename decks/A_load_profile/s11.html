A second idea: the 40/20/40 split per day

The idea

Apply 40/20/40 to each day, not the year: a hot day adds cooling up to the day’s base; a cold day, heating up to half of it.

Climate sets the split

Graded by the day’s degree-hours above 29°C / below 18°C. The budget stays 12,000 kWh; the yearly split now follows each city’s climate.

A first look

40 m², 15 kWh, year 0: Haifa’s peak drops from 112 to ~6 kWh/h; the battery adds more in every city.

Weak point at 29°C: too little HVAC is left (Haifa cooling ≈1.5%) — the threshold needs calibrating. A full answer needs the whole run matrix again.
