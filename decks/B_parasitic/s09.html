An idea for later: tie it to the battery

What bothered me

The parasitic load follows the home’s demand (5% of its cooling): a bigger home pays more, yet a 5 and a 20 kWh battery pay the same.

The idea

Tie it to the battery itself — its size and the energy that passes through it, at the room’s temperature.

What would change

Small batteries would pay less, large ones more. Not run — a full answer needs the whole run matrix again.

The logic in the thesis: a bigger home uses more, so its system stores more. The battery’s own throughput is the more direct measure.
